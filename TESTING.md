# In-game testing checklist

Use a Forever character with ordinary outdoor quests already in its log. Some checks need a particular quest type or a naturally full log. Run those when the situation occurs.

1. **Load and diagnose.** At character selection, enable Questionable Efficiency and the Forever release of QuestieDB. In the world, type `/qe show`, then `/qe diagnostics`. Expect Interface 16001 on the verified build, a matching quest count and capacity, and QuestieDB 1.1.6 in baked mode if using the verified release. The reverse chain index should move from building to ready. If the addon is missing, verify the exact folder and TOC placement and restart the client.
2. **Read the whole log.** Unwatch an existing quest and collapse its quest-log section. Under the addon's **Quest log** tab, confirm the quest remains present. Compare the count and cap against the game's own quest log. Accept a quest you were already going to take, then check that it appears.
3. **Check filters and order.** With quests of different levels, verify ascending route order and unknown levels after known ones. Check that a dungeon and raid quest appear under **Quest log** as excluded, and that an outdoor elite/group quest stays eligible with a group label. Use **Include** on an excluded quest, then **Clear override** and check that automatic classification returns.
4. **Check progress and locations.** Use a quest with two known objectives. Verify that both appear, that completing only one removes only that step, and that a turn-in replaces them once the game confirms the quest is ready. For an objective with several spawn areas, check that a plausible area is selected and that missing data is labeled. Turn in the quest manually and confirm it leaves both tabs.
5. **Try manual changes.** Click a quest title or select a route row. Verify the destination stays the same. Test **Do this next**, **Skip for now**, **Resume step**, **Exclude** and **Undo**. Turn in a quest affected by a manual action, then use **Undo**. Its steps should stay gone. Verify the reset confirmation describes what it clears, then confirm and undo the reset.
6. **Check a stable destination.** Walk toward a routed spawn or between two same-level objectives. The arrow and distance should update while the same target stays selected. Complete the current objective and verify the route advances. Use **Refresh route** and verify it can rescore from your current location.
7. **Fill and open a slot.** At the exact cap, verify **Full-log routing**. Compare a completed quest whose turn-in is in a revealed part of the map with a closer one whose turn-in sits under map fog. The revealed turn-in should be eligible, and the fogged one should show **Exploration ?** in **Quest log**. For a quest with both revealed and fogged spawns, make sure the addon picks a revealed one, including when the turn-in is still ahead. If no finish plan has all stops confirmed, check that the route explains the empty list. Reveal a previously blocked stop and check that the addon updates after the map unlocks or after **Refresh route**. Verify that **No follow-up recorded (unconfirmed)** is distinct from unavailable follow-up data. If a known follow-up is the best eligible option, check that the reason says to defer the offer. Turn in a quest manually and confirm that the mode returns to normal.
8. **Check native navigation outdoors.** While on the same continent as a known objective, face toward it and rotate 90 or 180 degrees. Check that the arrow follows your character's facing. Open the world map and look for the highlighted current pin and numbered upcoming pins. Use **Show on map** to preview another step, zoom to a continent, switch zones and test settings toggles. Check that existing pins and tracking from other addons are unaffected.
9. **Check unavailable navigation and the required dependency.** In a restricted area or with an objective on another continent, confirm the route remains visible when distance or facing is unavailable. On a test setup, temporarily disable QuestieDB at character selection and check that WoW reports a missing dependency for Questionable Efficiency. Re-enable QuestieDB before continuing.
10. **Check saved state.** Move the window, toggle the arrow and make a reversible route change. Use `/reload`. Confirm the window and settings are retained, and the still-valid manual choice is restored for this character.
11. **Prepare for a dungeon.** Enable **Include dungeon quests in routing** and confirm a dungeon quest becomes eligible while a raid quest stays excluded. Enable **Prepare for a dungeon**, check the default target of 5, and set a target above your current open slots. Confirm that the route prioritizes an explored outdoor turn-in. After reaching your target, confirm normal routing resumes. With fewer slots than the target, enter a dungeon and verify prep pauses, then resumes on leaving. Use `/reload` to check that both toggles and the slot target are saved.

## Collect a failure report

1. Reproduce the issue and record the checklist step and expected behavior.
2. Open `/qe diagnostics` and press **Ctrl+C**. Paste the report with the issue description. It contains no character or account identifiers.
3. If a Lua error dialog appeared, copy its message and stack. If you already use an error catcher, copy the QuestionableEfficiency error from it. Review raw error logs or screenshots for character, chat and account information before sharing.
4. If the addon never loaded, run `/dump GetBuildInfo()` and record the version, build and Interface alongside what the character-select AddOns list shows.
